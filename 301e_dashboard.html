Glossary terms used in this dashboard ▼
TermDefinition
IDB MM (#1545)Capital reserve account at Israel Discount Bank, money market. Funded by flip taxes and — through June 2026 — monthly shareholder assessments ($22,222/mo, now ended). Primary source for capital projects (LL11, intercom, plumbing, etc.).
IDB Escrow (#1561)RE tax reserve at Israel Discount Bank. Rebuilt monthly (~$31,333) from operating income; depleted quarterly when RE tax payments fall due (Jan, Apr, Jul, Oct).
FlagstarLender-required minimum balance under the co-op's mortgage covenant (~$85K). Cannot be freely drawn — it exists to satisfy the bank, not as a discretionary reserve.
Cap OneCapital One checking account. Day-to-day operating account — maintenance collections in, operating expenses out.
PMB (Prior Management Balance)Arrears balance carried over from a prior management period — arrears that predated current management reporting and are tracked separately in MRI.
LL11New York City Local Law 11 (Façade Inspection & Safety Program). Requires periodic inspection and repair of building exteriors. Our LL11 project is the façade repair completed by MSS Contracting.
APP6 / APP7IDB draw requisitions (Application for Payment). APP6 = 6th draw, APP7 = 7th draw. Each draw releases funds from IDB MM after work completion certification (G702/G703).
G702 / G703AIA standard contractor pay application forms. G702 is the summary; G703 is the schedule of values. Submitted by MSS to trigger each IDB draw.
RetainageA percentage of contract value (typically 10%) held back from each payment until substantial completion. Protects the owner if the contractor doesn't finish. Our retainage with MSS is ~$29,326.
TPP (Three Ten Plaza Partners)The neighbor at 310 E 52nd who shares a party wall. LL11 scaffold access required their cooperation; negotiation costs (legal + access fee) are part of the LL11 project total.
Covenant ReserveThe Flagstar minimum balance required under the mortgage agreement (~$85K). A contractual obligation, not a policy reserve. Falling below triggers a covenant breach.
